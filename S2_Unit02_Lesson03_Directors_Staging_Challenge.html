BRIEF· Introduce quickly — then return to staging and blocking Rostra & gauze
Two extra terms — keep them brief
Rostra
Rostra can create different heights and levels within a performance space. Optional rostra or safe level markers can be used if available. The main focus stays on staging and blocking.
Gauze
Gauze is a theatre material that can reveal, conceal or transform what the audience sees. Introduce the term, then return to today's staging choices.
Resources: chairs · floor markers · scenario cards · staging diagrams · optional rostra or safe level markers · written reflection sheet or digital form.

TEACHER· Use across all three lessons Support & extend
DIFFERENTIATION
Support
Display a labelled stage-area diagram · staging diagrams with the audience marked · scenario cards with beginning, event and consequence · role cards (director, performer, observer) · sentence starters for the written analysis · demonstrate understanding practically before writing · floor markers · one specific blocking target rather than several at once
Extend
