Knightswood Learning Community
Our faculty priorities

Consistent teaching. Sharper tracking. Stronger progress.

A practical one-page reference for the actions, checkpoints and evidence that matter most this session.
Core direction
Progress • Wellbeing • Inclusion
01
KEY FACULTY PRIORITY - Tracking & Moderation Publish the moderation calendar by September, complete at least four moderation activities, and have tracking frameworks in place for Art, Drama and Photography by October 2026.
Success looks like: consistent attainment judgements, clearer next steps for learners, and HMIE Q16 moving towards the 90% target.
02 • Learning & teaching

Knightswood Routines in every lesson

  • Embed all five Knightswood Routines consistently across the faculty.
  • Model routines and share Do Now resources at faculty meetings.
  • Use termly reviews of learning experiences alongside PT and peer observation evidence.
By November 2026: Do Now is consistently embedded and almost all learners experience all five routine elements in every lesson.
03 • Feedback, challenge & literacy

Better feedback. Better adaptation. Stronger evaluation.

  • Apply whole-school CPD on high quality feedback and adaptive teaching directly in lessons.
  • Complete two formal observations per teacher this session, with written feedback.
  • Use assessment evidence to adjust challenge and support.
  • Develop Drama's Evaluating organiser using shared vocabulary and modelled examples by November; adapt for Art by February 2027.
Impact targets: HMIE Q31 79% to 90%; HMIE Q20 76% to 90%; Drama Evaluating improves from baseline to end-of-session.
04 • Learner agency & curriculum

Make skills and progression visible

  • Update course plans by January 2027 so meta-skills, curricular skills and progression are explicit.
  • Build regular opportunities for learners to lead their learning, including PBL in S1/S2.
  • Use learner conversations and focus groups to test whether pupils can explain the skills they are developing.
Success looks like: almost all learners regularly lead aspects of learning; almost all S1/S2 learners can name and explain their meta-skills.
05 • Attainment & targeted support

Track the learners who need us most

  • Use ASN, staged intervention and transition information at P7-S1 and other key transition points.
  • Track the bottom 20% of S4 learners, S4 leavers and CE learners through attainment meetings and faculty intervention.
  • Support CE S4 learners to reach or exceed the Virtual Comparator.
By June 2027: targeted learners improve attainment, incoming learners are appropriately planned for, and faculty attainment sustains or improves on 2025 results.
06 • Wellbeing & inclusion

Safe, respected, included and able to participate

  • Apply Nurture Principles, monitor attendance termly and flag learners below 90% to Guidance.
  • Use restorative approaches; complete The Promise training and monitor CE attendance and attainment.
  • Complete the wider achievement audit by October and prioritise learners at risk of missing out.
  • Apply LCFE principles, keep classrooms calm and language-friendly, and remove financial barriers through Cost of the School Day approaches.
  • Keep families informed about exhibitions, performances and events.
Success looks like: attendance moves towards 90%; almost all learners feel safe and respected; wider achievement participation grows; SIMD 1 and 2 learners have full access to faculty activity.
