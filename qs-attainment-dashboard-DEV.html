      How to read this section
      • Chart 1 — How many pupils? Gold bars should shrink; teal bars should grow as Higher is withdrawn.
      • Chart 2 — Are they passing? Solid lines = % who got A, B or C. Compare gold to gold, teal to teal — not gold to teal.
      • Chart 3 — How strong are the grades? Average Insight points per pupil. Higher numbers = stronger grades. Teal uses a higher scale than gold — compare each line to its own past, not to the other colour.
      • Click any bar on these charts (or grade bars elsewhere) to open a pupil list with grades, components, attendance and SIMD.
