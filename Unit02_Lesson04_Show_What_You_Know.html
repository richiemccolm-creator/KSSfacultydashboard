MIME QUICK QUIZ.

Use what you can remember from the whole mime block. Tap a box when you want to reveal the answer.

1
What does S.P.E.C.S. stand for?
Simple · Precise · Exaggerated · Clear · Slow
2
Name three ways we communicate without speaking.
Facial expression · gesture · body language · posture · eye contact
3
What helps an invisible object look real?
Consistent size, shape, position, weight and grip
4
What is resistance?
Reacting as if an invisible force or surface is really pushing back
5
How can you show that something is heavy?
Use whole-body effort: knees, shoulders, arms, pace and facial expression
6
What is a mannerism?
A repeated physical habit that helps show character
7
What does status mean in Drama?
How powerful or important a character seems
8
Why should performers face the audience?
So the audience can read the action, expression and meaning clearly
9
What breaks the illusion of mime?
Talking, laughing, inconsistent objects, turning away, or unclear movement
10
What should every short mime have?
A clear beginning, a problem, a response and an ending

Assessment Rubric
Tap as you explain each level

HOW YOU WILL BE ASSESSED.

We are using the same tracking language you know: working on targets, not yet on track, on track and above expectations.

Creating & Working Together

1 · Working on targetsI needed lots of help to take part, share ideas or stay focused.
2 · Not yet on trackI took part and shared some ideas, but I did not always work well with my group or use rehearsal time well.
3 · On trackI worked well with my group, shared ideas and used rehearsal time properly.
4 · Above expectationsI helped shape the performance, supported others and helped the group improve it.

Mime & Movement Skills

1 · Working on targetsMy mime was often unclear and I need to use my face and body more.
2 · Not yet on trackSome of my mime was clear, but my movements were not always controlled or consistent.
3 · On trackMy mime was clear. I used movement, facial expression, gesture and body language well.
4 · Above expectationsMy mime was very clear and convincing. I used movement with strong control, detail and precision.

Performance Skills

1 · Working on targetsI sometimes forgot to face the audience, use the space or stay in role.
2 · Not yet on trackI stayed focused for some of the performance, but there were moments when the audience could not see or understand the action clearly.
3 · On trackI stayed focused, faced the audience and used the performance space well.
4 · Above expectationsI performed confidently, used the space very effectively and stayed completely focused throughout.

Impact on Audience

1 · Working on targetsThe audience found it difficult to understand what was happening.
2 · Not yet on trackThe audience understood some of the performance, but some moments were unclear.
3 · On trackThe audience could clearly understand the story, characters and action.
4 · Above expectationsThe performance was very clear, engaging and had a strong impact on the audience.

CAN AN AUDIENCE READ IT?

Halfway through rehearsal, stop and check whether the mime is genuinely clear.

What?What is happening?
Where?Where are you?
Who?Who are the characters?
Problem?What goes wrong?
Ending?How does it end?
If one of these is unclear, that is what your group fixes next.
