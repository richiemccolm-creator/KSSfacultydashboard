CONNECT TO MIME

MIME MAKES THE SIGNAL
STRONGER.

Mime communicates action, emotion and meaning through physical movement instead of spoken dialogue.

Bigger The audience can see it.
Slower The audience can read it.
Precise The audience understands it.
TECHNIQUE

MEET
S.P.E.C.S.

Use S.P.E.C.S. to make your mime readable to an audience.

SSimple · easy to follow.
PPrecise · exact movement.
EExaggerated · bigger than life.
CClear · meaning is obvious.
SSlow · time to read it.
