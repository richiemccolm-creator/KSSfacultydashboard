U01 · S06 · Group Report PreparationStay with your own advert
Phase 02 · Prepare to share

Turn your A3 map
into a 45-second report

Your taskStay in your group. Agree what the reporter will say. Everyone helps choose the strongest evidence.
1Name the job

“Our advert was for a ___ working for ___.”

2Explain the work

Choose the two most important duties.

3Identify the skill

Choose the skill the employer values most and point to evidence.

4Add one surprise

Share one requirement, condition or duty your group did not expect.

Finished: Reporter practises once. The rest of the group checks that every statement is supported by the advert.

Complete every line
for each opportunity

Field 02Company name

Field 04Employment type

Write responsibilities as clear bullet points in your own words. If pay is missing, write “not stated” and look for a reliable typical-pay source. Never invent information.

U01 · S06 · Research QualityUnderstand before you record
The quality test

Copy less.
Understand more.

Too weak

“A content creator makes content and posts it online.”

Evidence quality

“Plans short-form videos, films and edits content, schedules posts and checks audience data to improve future campaigns.”

Read the advert. Close the tab for a moment. Explain the role in your own words.
