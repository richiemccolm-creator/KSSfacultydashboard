RetrieveProcessUseUpdate
End-on
Audience on one side. Clear front. Like watching through an invisible fourth wall.
Thrust
Audience on three sides. Acting area extends into the audience.
In the round
Audience surrounds the performance. No single front.
Avenue / traverse
Audience on two opposite sides. Acting space runs between them.
Promenade
Audience moves between locations. Performers may guide them.

PROCESS· Establish this for every staging type Still 5–15 min

RetrieveProcessUseUpdate
Audience & performers

Front & sightlines

Audience effect
How could this arrangement affect the audience's experience?
Watch for
Thrust: performers must include several audience sections.
In the round: blocking and sightlines need careful attention.
Avenue: performers may easily block one audience side.
Promenade: transitions and audience management become important.
