New Idea
Status

LOW. NEUTRAL. HIGH.

Status is the level of power, confidence or importance a character seems to have.

Low statusSmaller, closed, avoiding, uncertain.
NeutralBalanced, relaxed, ordinary.
High statusOpen, still, confident, takes space.
Ask: nobody spoke, so why do we read them differently?
Challenge 02
Same action, different status

CHANGE THE STATUS. KEEP THE ACTION.

Do the same simple action three times: enter the room, cross the space, sit down.

Start hereMake low, neutral and high status clearly different.
Level upUse eye contact, posture and pace deliberately.
Take it furtherShift status halfway through without speaking.
QUESTION · WHICH VERSION TOOK UP THE MOST SPACE?QUESTION · WHICH VERSION FELT MOST POWERFUL?

ONE SMALL HABIT CAN SAY A LOT.

A mannerism is a repeated movement or habit that helps the audience read a character.

Check timeImpatient or anxious?
FidgetNervous or distracted?
Adjust clothesSelf-conscious or proud?
Avoid eye contactShy, guilty or unsure?
