# StageFeed
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
React, TypeScript, Vite, Dexie and jsPDF as requested. Local-only application.
## Users
Scottish secondary pupils S1–S6 authoring fictional character accounts and theatre design portfolios; teachers reviewing portable submissions.
## Product Purpose
A realistic private social simulation where pupils express dramatic interpretation and creative development through posts, stories and fictional conversations.
## Constraints
No accounts, external messaging, analytics or cloud storage. Images processed locally. Multiple projects persist in IndexedDB; backups preserve media. All counts and conversations are fictional. Accessible mobile, tablet and desktop layouts.
## Brand commitments
StageFeed; dark default, charcoal and subtle purple, original identity and social profile interface. No mock-up was present in the attachment directory.
## Principles
The social experience leads. Educational support is optional. Local save status must be truthful. User-entered text renders safely. Demonstration content stays distinct from pupil projects.
